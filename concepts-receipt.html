Receipts · UI concept 02

Pump 04 · Thermal

A gas station receipt, done properly. Thermal paper on a grey counter, black and red printer ink, everything set in monospace, and the card terminal's own words: APPROVED, DECLINED. Entries feed out of the printer as they arrive, points sit on a pump display, and the board is the whole night's receipt, barcode and all.

Try it

Tap Approve on Kev's entry for the stamp. Press return in the comment box to post, or tap a comment to reply. Replay the board to watch the night print. Step the points on the pump.

Motion and feel

Smooth, not cheap

  • Print. New receipts feed down out of the printer, one after another on first load.
  • Stamp. When your vote settles an entry, APPROVED or DECLINED is stamped on in red.
  • Press. Everything you tap gives a little under your thumb and springs back.
  • Light tap on every press.
  • Success when you approve, warning when you decline.
  • Soft tick on each pump step and player pick.
  • Pump digits count and roll to new values instead of jumping.
  • Headers frost and shrink once content scrolls beneath them.
  • Reduce Motion on the phone turns all of it into simple fades.
